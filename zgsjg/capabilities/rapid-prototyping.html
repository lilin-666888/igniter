02 · 5-Step Process

From drawing to prototype in your hand.

Five steps, total elapsed time 7–14 days for most geometries. Every step has a named owner on our engineering team — you won't be passed between departments.

📐
Step 01 — Drawing Upload

Send a STEP file (preferred), PDF, or DWG to engineering@ceramitell.com. Include material preference if you have one, target quantity, and end application. Tip: rougher info is better than no info — we'd rather see "rough sketch + concept" than wait.

Accept → STEP · PDF · DWG · Hand sketch
🔍
Step 02 — DFM Review (48h)

Your drawing goes to a dedicated DFM engineer (named, not a queue). They check manufacturability, material fit, and tolerances. Reply within 48 hours with feedback, alternative suggestions, and material recommendations.

Output → DFM feedback · material rec
💲
Step 03 — Quote Confirmed

Same 48-hour reply includes quotation for prototype units and indicative pricing for production volumes. Quote valid for 30 days. NDA can be signed before quote if your drawing is proprietary.

Output → Prototype + production quote
🔨
Step 04 — Prototype Build (7–14d)

On PO receipt, your part enters our prototype cell. Forming → sintering → grinding → inspection. For standard custom geometries: 7–10 days. Complex (thin walls, deep features, tight tolerance): 14–21 days.

Output → 1–5 prototype units
📦
Step 05 — Lot Cert + Ship

Each prototype ships with a lot certificate (dimensional inspection, material grade verification, surface finish measurement). Standard packaging is foam-cradle inside dimensional shipping crate. DHL/FedEx Express to anywhere in 42 export markets.

Output → Prototype + lot cert + DHL
🚀

03 · Engineering FAQ

Questions about our process.

Direct answers about how the prototype cell works. Email engineering@ceramitell.com with anything we haven't covered.

What file formats do you accept?
STEP (preferred), IGES, DWG, DXF, PDF with dimensions, or even a hand sketch with critical dimensions called out. For new projects, a STEP file always cuts 1–2 days off the DFM cycle vs PDF.
Do I need to know the material before submitting?
No — describe the application (operating temperature, mechanical load, chemical environment) and we'll recommend a material. About 30% of incoming projects come to us with the "wrong" material spec'd; DFM review catches this before tooling.
Will you sign an NDA before reviewing my drawing?
Yes — mutual NDAs are standard for proprietary drawings. We can use our template (signed within 1 business day) or your template (2–3 days for legal review). NDA signing doesn't delay the 48-hour DFM clock.
What's the cost difference between prototype and production?
Prototype units are typically 5–10× production unit cost — reflecting one-off tooling, manual finishing, and inspection effort. Prototype charges credit against the first production order if production proceeds within 6 months of prototype delivery.
Can you ship internationally?
Yes — we ship to 42 countries via DHL, FedEx, and UPS Express. EU customs handled by our EU-based forwarder (typical 3–5 business days door-to-door). North America: 4–7 business days door-to-door. Asia Pacific: 2–4 business days.
What's the MOQ for production after prototype approval?
MOQ varies by complexity: 50–100 units for standard machined parts, 500+ for custom-tooled production runs, 5,000+ for fully custom igniter SKUs. We can discuss volume-pricing tier breaks during the DFM call.
